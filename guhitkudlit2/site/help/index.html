    How baybayin works

    Baybayin is an abugida: each consonant character carries a built-in “a” sound. There are three vowels:

    and fourteen consonants:

    A mark called the kudlit changes the vowel. Above the character, it makes an “e/i” sound. Below, it makes an “o/u” sound. A final mark after the character removes the vowel entirely.

    Letters without a baybayin character

    Baybayin has no characters for C, F, J, Q, V, X, and Z. Spell the word the way it sounds in the Tagalog alphabet (abakada) instead.

    How your text is converted

    • E is written as I, and O is written as U. They share a kudlit.
    • R is written with the DA character.
    • Repeated letters are written once, e.g. Anna becomes Ana.
    • ng and mga are written with their traditional spellings.
    • Accents are dropped, and punctuation is ignored.
